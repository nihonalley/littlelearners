Free Learning Games for Toddlers and Preschoolers

Little Learners is a collection of simple interactive learning games designed for children ages 2–4. Each activity uses large touch-friendly choices, spoken instructions, short play sessions and gentle feedback to make early learning easy to enjoy on phones, tablets and computers.
